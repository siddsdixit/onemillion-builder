How It Works

Five files a day.
Your AI does the build.

You are the director. Claude Code (or Cursor, or any AI tool) is the builder. You spec, verify, and ship.

📖
Read the concept (learn.md)

~10–20 min. The mental model behind what you're building. Named examples. No jargon without definition.

🔨
Follow the build (build.md)

Step-by-step instructions. Every command has expected output. Every error has a fix. No "it should work."

🤖
Paste the verifier (ai-instructions.md)

Paste one prompt into your AI tool. Claude reads your project, runs checks, reports pass or needs revision.

🎬
Watch Sid do it (loom.md)

A 10–15 min screen recording per day. Sid hits the same errors you'll hit. He leaves the mistakes in.
