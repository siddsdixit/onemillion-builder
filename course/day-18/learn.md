# Day 18: Launch

**Time: 20 minutes | Phase: SELL**

---

## Learn (5 min)

The SELL agent is a Head of Growth Marketing. It writes actual launch assets — not templates, not advice — ready-to-post content. It also runs an SEO audit on your live URL and generates an investor deck PDF.

The philosophy: 10 users who love you are worth more than 1,000 who don't care. Launch doesn't mean everyone knows about you. Launch means the first real users can find you.

Today you'll post about your product for the first time.

## Do (10 min)

1. Run the sell agent:
   ```
   Continue to sell
   ```

2. The agent produces:
   - Landing page copy (headline, problem, solution, CTA)
   - A ready-to-post X/Twitter thread
   - A LinkedIn post
   - An HN "Show HN" post
   - Cold email templates
   - 5-10 communities to post in
   - Investor deck PDF

3. Post the X/Twitter thread. Right now. Don't edit it for 30 minutes first.

4. Submit to at least one community (HN, relevant subreddit, indie hacker forum).

5. Review the SEO audit:
   ```bash
   cat .onemillion/seo-audit.md
   ```

## Reflect (5 min)

**Assignment:** You built and shipped a product in 18 days. Write your origin story: what problem, what solution, what did you learn, what surprised you. This goes on your landing page About section.
