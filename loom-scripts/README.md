# Loom Scripts — OneMillion

Scripts for every Loom you'll record. Read once, record in ~15-20 min each.

---

## Production Philosophy (Same For Every Loom)

1. **Show before explain.** Open with the end result. Show it working in the first 30 seconds. Then explain how it's done.
2. **30 seconds per concept.** If you can't explain a concept in 30 seconds, you're at the wrong level of detail.
3. **One thing per slide / per moment.** Don't try to cover everything. Cover ONE thing well.
4. **Leave mistakes in.** If you fumble a command, leave it. If you hit a real error, react out loud and fix it on camera. The "Classic. Here's why." moment beats any polished take.
5. **Energy over production quality.** A slightly rough Loom with high energy beats a polished, low-energy one every time.
6. **Show your face.** Builds trust. Even when you're switching to VS Code, keep face cam in corner.
7. **End with a clear next step.** Every Loom closes with what they should do right now.

---

## Gear (One-Time Setup)

**Minimum viable:**
- Loom Desktop app (Cmd+Shift+L on Mac to start)
- Built-in laptop camera + mic
- Screen + face camera both ON

**Recommended upgrades (worth it):**
- A USB-C microphone (Blue Yeti, $100; or Rode Lavalier, $50)
- Decent lighting — face the window OR get a $30 ring light
- Quiet room or noise-canceling headphones

**Total upgrade cost: $50-150. Worth it if you'll record 18+ Looms.**

## Pre-Recording Checklist (Same For Every Loom)

Before hitting record:

- [ ] Close all browser tabs not needed for the demo
- [ ] Silence phone + computer notifications (Mac: Focus mode → Do Not Disturb)
- [ ] Close Slack, email, Discord
- [ ] Have a fresh test email ready if you'll be signing up
- [ ] Camera + mic test: record 5 sec, watch it back, adjust if needed
- [ ] Quiet environment (turn off fans, close door)
- [ ] Reference the script next to your screen (printed or on phone — NOT covering Loom)
- [ ] Glass of water nearby

---

## Recording Workflow

1. Hit record in Loom (Cmd+Shift+L)
2. Wait 2 seconds before speaking (gives editor buffer)
3. Cold open (the first 10-15 seconds — most important part)
4. Work through the script
5. Wait 2 seconds at the end before stopping
6. Stop recording
7. Loom auto-uploads → copy public link
8. Paste link into the day's `loom.md` file in this repo
9. Commit + push

**Target lengths:**
- Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 (concept days): 5-7 min
- Day 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11 (code days): 10-15 min
- Day 7, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18 (lighter days): 5-8 min
- Day 9, 13, 16: 8-10 min

## Daily Ritual Loom (Bonus — Move 4 from retention strategy)

Once per day during Cohort 0, record a **60-second** Loom:

```
"Good morning. Today is Day X of OneMillion. Here's what we're doing:
[one sentence].

Here's what I built yesterday: [show one thing].

Tag me when you finish today's day. Let's go."
```

60 seconds. Unedited. Posted to LinkedIn + the cohort channel. **Builds the ritual** that keeps people showing up.

Total cost: 10 min/day × 18 days = 3 hours over a cohort. Highest leverage daily work.

## After Recording: Where Looms Live

Each day's Loom URL goes in `[day-X]/loom.md`:

```markdown
# Day X — Loom Video

[Embedded Loom — link below]

Recording URL: https://www.loom.com/share/abc123def456

### What's covered
[...current placeholder content stays...]
```

Loom URLs become permanent course assets. Don't delete or unlist them.
